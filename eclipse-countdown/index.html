Eclipse Countdown

A place name is looked up with Open-Meteo's keyless geocoder — the only thing this tool sends anywhere, and only when you press the button. Coordinates are read here and go nowhere. The eclipse arithmetic runs entirely in your browser, either way.

Or did you mean
Say where you are and this counts down to the next solar eclipse there. The disc is the Sun as your sky will have it, zenith up — the Moon coming in from the west, the bite growing, the sky going out if you are standing in the shadow's path — and beside it is the time to the next thing that happens, and the whole running order. Nothing here is a guess about the weather: it is where the Moon will be, which has been known for a long time.
