Eclipse Countdown

Place names are resolved by Open-Meteo's keyless geocoder, the only request this tool makes, and only when you press the button. Coordinates are parsed here and sent nowhere. The eclipse calculations run in your browser either way.

Other matches
Enter a place or a pair of coordinates. This finds the next solar eclipse visible from there, counts down to its next phase, and lists the times of all of them. The disc shows the Sun as it will look from that spot, drawn with the zenith up for the moment on the clock.

    The elements, the calculations and the catalogue come from Eclipse Recon, which adds the map, the terrain and the cloud forecast. Contact times assume a smooth lunar limb at sea level, so times at the very edge of the path can be a few seconds out. An eclipse pasted into Recon from its NASA page appears here too.
