      Assessment

      how the score is computed score = 100 · √(t/tmax) · vis² · √(alt/8°) · sky0.75

      Factors are weighted by certainty. The horizon is surveyed fact, so its visible fraction counts squared — half of totality behind terrain costs three quarters of the score, fully blocked is 0 whatever the sky. Below 8° the Sun stands in many air masses, where haze and horizon murk live: geometry, so it counts with the certain factors. The sky is a forecast, so its middle is softened — but under 20 it falls quadratically, and a certain storm is 0. Duration counts gently: half the maximum is still most of the show. For a Sun under 20° the sky number is the worse of overhead and the sightline decks toward its azimuth.
